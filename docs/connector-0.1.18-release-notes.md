# Otterin Connector 0.1.18 — Stop shows Interrupted

## Changed
- After you tap Stop on your iPhone, steps Devin was in the middle of now show "Interrupted" instead of "Failed".
- The pairing hint in the Connector window now matches the iPhone app: Your computer → Pair a computer → Scan pairing code.

## Install
1. Choose Quit Otterin Connector from the menu-bar menu.
2. Open the DMG and drag Otterin Connector into Applications.
3. Replace the existing Otterin Connector app when prompted.
4. Open Otterin Connector.

Apple Silicon macOS 13 or newer. Developer ID signed, Apple notarized and stapled.
